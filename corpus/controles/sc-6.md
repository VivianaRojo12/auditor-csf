# SC-6 Resource Availability

- Control: SC-6
- Familia: SC — Protección de sistemas y comunicaciones
- Función CSF 2.0: PR (Proteger)
- Fuente: NIST SP 800-53 5.2.0

## Control

Protect the availability of resources by allocating [resources] by [priority / quota / {{ insert: param, sc-06_odp.03 }} ].

## Guía complementaria

Priority protection prevents lower-priority processes from delaying or interfering with the system that services higher-priority processes. Quotas prevent users or processes from obtaining more than predetermined amounts of resources.
